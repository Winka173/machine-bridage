# Muzzle audit

Generated by `MachineBrigade.Editor.MuzzleGeometryAudit.Report` (DECISIONS 13E); `MuzzleAuditTests` enforces it.

Every weapon mount of every armed vehicle, elite, boss and tower, spawned through the game's own path (`ModelLibrary`'s template and launch points, `VehicleView.MuzzleOf`'s slot mapping) and measured against the model's own triangles in the muzzle's moving group (its turret, weapon mount or boss part; the whole model for a hull or aircraft gun).

- **Ahead of tip:** where the muzzle sits along the drawn barrel's line against the open end of its barrel, tube or pod (+ ahead of it, - inside it), drawn metres, worst over the mount's points. Allowed -0.08 to +0.15.
- **Off axis:** off the barrel's middle at its open end, drawn metres (allowed 0.05, or half the barrel's radius). On a face of tubes, where rounds leave from spots all over it, the point only has to be on the face (`face`).
- **Axis:** the barrel's own axis (its middle at the tip and further back) against the line the flash is drawn along, degrees (allowed 6).
- **Points:** how many different points the mount fires from (pods, rails, twin barrels, spots on a face).
- **Part:** the model part the barrel's walls belong to at its open end.
