- troop/cargo transport gameplay;
- recovery/towing;
- revive/resurrection;
- salvage;

- a new radar/vision gameplay subsystem;

- ship movement/navigation;
- weapons/projectiles;
- SAM;
- CIWS/interception;
- jammer/aura;
- shield;

## D. Role implementation

### torpedo_boat
- cheap anti-heavy glass cannon;
- Armour 0–1;
- HP about 1.15–1.30 × current missile_boat HP;
- primary `naval_torpedo_light`;
- torpedo visibly slower than AShM;
- high anti-heavy value;
- weak vs gunboat/corvette/air.

### ciws_escort_craft
- missile/drone interception;
- Armour 1;
- HP around hover_gunboat or slightly higher;
- poor surface DPS;
- weaker point defence than high-tier dedicated systems.

### ashm_corvette
- Armour 1–2;
- HP ~0.85–0.95 × sea_corvette;
- primary `anti_ship_missile_corvette`;
- Pen 4;
- projectile speed ~80 m/s;
- light gun only as secondary.

### aa_corvette
- short/medium SAM + CIWS;
- no strong anti-surface alpha;
- SAM damage follows chosen player family and is not generically buffed.

### ew_corvette
- reuse existing jammer/aura semantics;
- no radar/reveal/fake contacts;
- light self-defence gun only;
- weak in direct duel.

### frigate
- Armour 2;
- HP ~1.20–1.35 × sea_corvette;
- 100–127 mm main gun;
- one moderate secondary capability only;
- generalist, not specialist-best.

### missile_frigate
- stronger AShM salvo;
- modest gun;
- weak AA;
- clear long cooldown.

### aa_frigate
- medium SAM + CIWS;
- modest surface weapon;
- no heavy AShM alpha.

### rocket_artillery_ship
- naval MLRS/GMLRS using existing fire-support rules;
- respect minimum range;
- use locked projectile speeds;
- differentiate by coverage/cadence, not arbitrary raw damage inflation.

### naval_monitor
- Armour 3;
- slow;
- high HP for cost;
- 152–203 mm primary;
- strong vs structure/medium ships;
- weak vs AShM/air.

### destroyer
- Armour 3;
- HP ~1.35–1.50 × frigate;
- 127–130 mm main gun;
- moderate AShM + short/medium SAM + limited CIWS;
- none of those secondary systems equal specialist variants.

### gun_destroyer
- same tier durability;
- main gun effective output +10–15% over base destroyer;
- reduce missile capability;
- do NOT buff all secondary guns/MGs.

### missile_destroyer
- large AShM salvo;
- weaker gun;
- self-defence SAM only;
- readable/interceptable salvo.

### aa_destroyer
- medium/long player SAM + CIWS;
- modest surface gun;
- weak relative to surface specialists when isolated;
- player SAM speeds use latest player missile master, not boss overrides.

### sea_cruiser.gun
- Armour 3–4;
- 152–203 mm heavy gun;
- limited SAM/CIWS;
- strong vs destroyer/structure.

### sea_cruiser.missile
- Armour 3;
- heavy AShM/cruise-style player weapon;
- medium SAM + CIWS at non-specialist strength;
- reduced main gun;
- do not use boss cruise weapons directly.

### battlecruiser
- Armour 3–4;
- HP between cruiser and battleship;
- faster than battleship;
- choose heavy gun OR heavy missile emphasis as primary;
- the other remains secondary;
- do not combine battleship durability with missile-cruiser alpha.

### battleship
- CP 23;
- Armour 4;
- highest normal player naval HP;
- 280–406 mm primary gun;
- long reload;
- secondary AA/CIWS not specialist-tier;
- no aircraft spawn/transport/repair aura gimmick;
- still vulnerable to concentrated Pen4 AShM/torpedo/specialist fire.
