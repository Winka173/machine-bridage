## 15. Boss part states

`BossPartStates`: Healthy / Damaged (< 0.5) / Critical (< 0.25) / Destroyed — thresholds already used by part

## 16. Audio priority table

`Game/Audio/AudioPolicy.cs`: classes P0–P5 over `SoundPriority` (new `Critical` = 80 = P0 for big whistles/super
cues; `Warning` = P1), each with its own max instances, cooldown, distance weight, virtualisation and bus.
`VoiceArbiter` (pure function): a playing P0/P1 voice is never taken by anything else; a P0/P1 sound always gets a
voice, even past its bank's instance limit when the bank is all active warnings. New `CriticalWarnings` bus with
its own Settings slider ("Warnings", 20–100 %, `mb.warningVolume`), separated from the general effects slider.

## 17. Voice virtualization policy

Bounded per class (see §16); bank cooldown no longer drops a P1 cue at another spot more than 12 m away; P0/P1
cues carry 25 % farther than ordinary effects; P0/P1 are never dropped from a full (24-deep) schedule queue — a
lesser pending sound is dropped instead; the one-hiss-per-gap thinning rule applies to far hisses only (an
incoming missile hiss is never thinned); warnings are never occluded (must stay intelligible, `AudioDirector.Space`).

## 18. Occlusion / distance implementation

**Occlusion** (`AudioDirector.Space`): near (< 70 m), P2/P3 voices only, listener→source line checked against the
Sim's `CoverGrid` (tall props, walls) every 0.25 s per voice, capped at 6 queries/frame, smoothed; gain and
low-pass driven by the zone's occlusion material. **Distance layers** (`Bank.Layered`, large-weapon banks only):
near transient (< 0.25 of reach, full band), body (to 0.6, closing to 5 kHz), far report (to 1.1 of reach, 16 %
level floor so it never fades out like a rifle, pitch 0.96) plus a per-voice `AudioEchoFilter` for the zone's
environment tail. **Acoustic zones** (`AcousticZones.Build`): the map cut into 16 m cells by theme/CoverGrid/
terrain tags into 6 profiles (Open, UrbanStreet, Fortress, Forest, Harbor, Snowfield + Interior reserved), each
with its own reverb preset, occlusion material, ambient profile and far-report tail, read at the listener in O(1).

## 19. Accessibility changes

Camera shake: 0–100 % in 10 % steps (old Off/Low/Full map to 0/35/100 %) plus a fatigue limiter (after 2 s of
unbroken shake, new shake counts down to 35 %). "Reduced effects": cosmetic blast additions and thrown chunks at
half, flash/core fire/rings/danger shapes stay whole (the game has no screen distortion to begin with); full-screen
blast wash clamped to 2/s, a quarter of that with Reduced flashes. "Reduced flashes" (from W1-B): big-gun muzzle
flash ×0.7, fire glow without fast flicker. Warning captions (Settings, off by default): `CueCaptions` reads
`CueFeed`, 3 lines, merges repeats with a count, an arrow for an off-screen source, P0 lines get a thick amber bar;
small-arms fire is never captioned (radio speech is already text).
