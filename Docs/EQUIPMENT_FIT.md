# Equipment fit matrix

Generated by `EquipmentDocsExport` (MB_BALANCE=1) from the code and balance.json: do not edit by hand.

Rules (prompt 8 I.1): every card says what it has from its weapons and body (`VehicleFit.Of`); every piece, stat line, unique line, module and brand says what it needs. A class meets a need when at least a third of its cards do; a branch fits a piece when one of its classes meets the piece's need (the base type and its unique line together). Crates only drop pieces that fit at least one branch, and the equipment screen only lets a piece onto a branch it fits (old saves: unfit pieces go back to the inventory, `PlayerProfile.MigrateFit`).
