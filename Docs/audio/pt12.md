Numbers: momentary max LUFS (the loudest 400 ms) from `python Tools/sfx/analyze_sfx.py`. "Effective" = the clip's level

lived in those levels. The machine guns came up 9.6 dB and the autocannons 6.9 dB while the big guns the owner likes came

fill the mix and the compressor. Their new clips were duller (no bright body, no bolt clack) and the autocannon's 0.9 s
recorded clip stuttered under the voice limit. The missiles got a gun's thump and echo on a pitched-down recording.

Every launch is a rocket motor, not a gun: ignition crack; the booster's band noise with a 20-60 Hz flutter and sparse
crackle impulses; its cutoff gliding from 7.5 kHz to 1.1 kHz as it flies off; the sustainer's 2.5-9 kHz hiss; a low exhaust
rumble (noise, never a falling sine); a diffuse dull air tail (never discrete slap-back taps). All synthesised in
`Tools/sfx/build_sfx.py` (no recordings, no AI); the old recorded launch clips were the base of L7's tank-like boom.
